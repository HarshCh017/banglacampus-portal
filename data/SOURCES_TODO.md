## 3. Regulatory Verification Guidelines (AICTE / NAAC / NIRF)
1. **NIRF Ranking Band**: Must be cross-referenced against `nirfindia.org` 2024 Engineering Band listings.
2. **NAAC Grade**: Must cite the valid accreditation cycle date from `naac.gov.in`.
3. **NBA Accreditation**: Must specify exact program branches accredited (e.g. CSE, ECE) from `nbaind.org`.
