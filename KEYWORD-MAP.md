## 2. FUNNEL STAGE MAPPING

1. **Top of Funnel (Discovery / Brand)**:
   - Keywords: `I.E.M Kolkata`, `Institute of Engineering and Management`, `I.E.M courses`.
   - Content: Strong institutional authority, AICTE/MAKAUT autonomous status, campus facilities, verified achievements.
2. **Middle of Funnel (Evaluation / Trust)**:
   - Keywords: `I.E.M B.Tech fees`, `I.E.M placements`, `IEMJEE eligibility`, `I.E.M CSE`.
   - Content: Transparent fee schedules, verified recruiter lists, salary reality, curriculum comparison.
3. **Bottom of Funnel (Conversion / Qualification)**:
   - Keywords: `I.E.M B.Tech admission`, `IEMJEE application`, `I.E.M eligibility`, `I.E.M direct admission`.
   - Content: 4-field guidance form, preliminary percentage indication, counsellor callback SLA.

## 3. ENTITY DISAMBIGUATION RULES FOR SEO
- In West Bengal, "IEM" is colloquially paired with sister institute "UEM" (University of Engineering & Management, New Town) and other institutes using similar acronyms.
- **On-Page Safeguards**:
  1. Every page `<title>` must contain: `[Page Topic] | Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM), Kolkata`.
  2. Organization Schema: `legalName: "Institute of Engineering & Management"`, `alternateName: ["IEM Kolkata", "IEM Salt Lake"]`, `disambiguatingDescription: "Autonomous engineering college affiliated with MAKAUT located in Sector V, Salt Lake, Kolkata."`.
  3. Physical NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistently displayed on footer and contact blocks.
