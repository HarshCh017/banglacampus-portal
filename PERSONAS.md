## 1. PERSONA 1: THE PROSPECTIVE STUDENT
*Aspiring Engineer seeking clarity, authentic branch fit, and simple next steps.*

- **Demographics**: 17–19 years old; finishing or completed Class 12 (CBSE / ISC / WBCHSE / State Boards).
- **Primary Device**: Mobile-first (Android / iPhone, 360px–430px screens; 4G/5G).
- **Session Context**: Browsing between study sessions or entrance exam prep; short attention span; low tolerance for multi-step walls, unclickable PDFs, or aggressive OTP popups.

### Jobs-to-Be-Done (JTBD)
1. **Preliminary Eligibility Indication**: "Does my Class 12 aggregate or PCM percentage meet the published threshold for CSE or specialized tech branches at IEM?"
2. **Branch Specialization Clarity**: "What is the practical difference between Core CSE, AI & ML, IoT, Cyber Security, and IT?"
3. **Entrance Pathway Understanding**: "Do I need to take IEMJEE, WBJEE, or JEE Main? When is the IEMJEE exam, and is there an application fee?" (Verified: IEMJEE has no exam fee).
4. **Actionable Next Step**: "What exact step do I take right now to secure my seat or get in touch with an advisor?"

### Emotional Anxieties & Doubts
- Fear of being misled by aggregator sites into entering phone/email only to be spammed by irrelevant private colleges.
- Confusion over WBJEE counselling rank cutoffs versus direct/IEMJEE admission options.
- Skepticism toward generic "100% placement" claims without proof of recruiting companies or salary distribution.

### Decision Criteria
- Directness and honesty of answers.
- Fast, instant feedback without forced phone verification gates.
- Recognition that IEM is one of the premier private engineering institutions in Kolkata / Eastern India.

### On-Page Journey Path
`Hero (Outcome headline) → Quick Branch Indication (4 fields) → Branch Difference Cards → IEMJEE Exam Timeline → Submit Guidance Lead → WhatsApp / Call Confirmation`.

## 2. PERSONA 2: THE PARENT
*Pragmatic Sponsor evaluating legitimacy, institutional credibility, financial investment, and student safety.*

- **Demographics**: 40–55 years old; salaried professional, business owner, or educator.
- **Primary Device**: Desktop/Laptop (work hours / evening) + WhatsApp on mobile for sharing and follow-ups.
- **Session Context**: Thorough, deliberate scrutiny; cross-referencing information with relatives, official bodies, and alumni; reading fine print.

### Jobs-to-Be-Done (JTBD)
1. **Accreditation & Legitimacy Proof**: "Is the degree awarded by MAKAUT? Is the college AICTE-approved, NAAC-accredited, and NBA-accredited?"
2. **Comprehensive Fee Transparency**: "What is the exact semester-by-semester fee? Are there hidden uniform, lab, or exam costs? Are bank education loan demand letters available?"
3. **Campus Environment & Safety**: "Where is the campus located in Salt Lake Sector V? What are the hostel facilities and anti-ragging compliance?"
4. **Placement Realism**: "Which reputable companies visit IEM campus (e.g. TCS, Cognizant, Wipro, Capgemini, PwC)? What is the verifiable median/average package?"

### Emotional Anxieties & Doubts
- High anxiety about investing ₹8L–₹10L+ in private engineering education without guaranteed quality of education.
- Fear of unauthorized third-party brokers or fraudulent admission agents claiming fake quota seats.
- Confusion regarding the administrative distinction between autonomous IEM Kolkata and the sister university (UEM Kolkata).

### Decision Criteria
- Transparent institutional disclosures (official addresses: Ashram Building, Sector V; landlines: 033-2357 2995; official emails).
- Formal loan facilitation guidance (IOB bank account details and accounts department contact).
- Absence of hyperbolic or predatory sales tactics.

### On-Page Journey Path
`Sticky Header / Tel Call → Institutional Accreditations Strip (AICTE / MAKAUT / NAAC) → Fee & Scholarship Section → Placement Audit Details → Request Official Guidance Lead → WhatsApp Follow-up with Senior Counsellor`.

## 3. PERSONA 3: THE LATERAL ENTRY APPLICANT
*Polytechnic Diploma Holder looking for 2nd Year B.Tech direct admission pathways.*

- **Demographics**: 19–22 years old; completed or in final semester of 3-year Engineering Diploma (WBSCTE or other state polytechnics) or B.Sc with Mathematics.
- **Primary Device**: Mobile / Tablet.
- **Session Context**: Highly goal-oriented; comparing 2nd-year lateral seat availability across West Bengal colleges post-JELET examination.

### Jobs-to-Be-Done (JTBD)
1. **Lateral Availability Confirmation**: "Does IEM Kolkata offer lateral entry into B.Tech 2nd year for my diploma branch?"
2. **Eligibility Requirements**: "What is the minimum diploma percentage required (e.g. AICTE 45% / 40% reserved)? Is WB JELET mandatory?"
3. **Fees & Duration**: "What is the fee for the 3-year duration (3rd to 8th semesters)?"
4. **Counselling vs Direct Process**: "How are decentralized or institutional quota seats allotted post-WBJEEB JELET counselling?"

### Emotional Anxieties & Doubts
- Lack of clear information on college websites, where lateral entry is often treated as an afterthought or omitted entirely.
- Worry about credit transfer, curriculum bridging, and placement eligibility alongside 4-year regular students.

### Decision Criteria
- Clear, dedicated lateral entry guidance and explicit status (whether JELET-dependent or institutional).
- Transparent branch eligibility matrix for diploma holders.

### On-Page Journey Path
`Dedicated Lateral Entry Section/Page → Diploma Eligibility Checker → Verified Seat & Fee Guidance → Connect with Lateral Entry Admission Officer`.
